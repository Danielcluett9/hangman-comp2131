How To Play

  • A secret word is chosen at random. Read the hint for a clue.
  • Type a letter in the box and choose Guess, or click a letter button.
  • Correct letters appear in the word. Wrong letters add a part to the drawing.
  • Six wrong guesses and the game is lost. Find every letter first to win!
